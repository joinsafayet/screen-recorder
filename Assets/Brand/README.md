# Prism identity

The folded P combines the app initial with an open capture aperture and an optical refraction edge. Cobalt provides recognition, sea green identifies the refracted face, and navy anchors the macOS tile.

## Deliverables

- `PrismMark.png`: 1254 × 1254 transparent standalone logo master.
- `PrismAppIcon.png`: 1254 × 1254 transparent macOS icon artwork, with a navy rounded tile.
- `build/Prism.app/Contents/Resources/AppIcon.icns`: exact 16/32/64/128/256/512/1024 pixel representations generated by the app build.
- `Sources/Prism/PrismIcons.swift`: 30 custom interface glyphs drawn as native SwiftUI vectors, plus native system status/transport symbols. Main icons use a 24-point grid, rounded joins/caps, and 1.75-point nominal strokes. Small library/detail glyphs simplify below 16 points. Icons inherit the app's existing selected/disabled/status colors.

Keep the mark's negative space open, preserve all facets, and allow generous clear space. The sidebar uses a 42-point tile. Utility symbols stay monochrome; brand color identifies selection rather than decorating every icon. Recording, warning, success, and disabled states retain distinct meaning. The mark loads once per process, and the vector icons introduce no idle animation loop. The icon pipeline resamples approved artwork at exact pixel dimensions with transparent margins.

## Brand colors

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cobalt | `#346BEB` | Primary brand face |
| Sea green | `#35BCA5` | Refracted edge |
| Sapphire | `#2350A5` | Fold depth |
| Ink navy | `#101D32` | App icon tile |

UI accent variants remain user-selectable. Light and dark interfaces keep their own accessible foreground colors; they do not force the logo's saturated colors onto body text.

## Artwork method and prompts

The raster logo/app icon were created with the built-in image generation tool. The interface glyphs are deterministic native vector paths. Generated artwork lives in this repository, with the original generation output retained separately.

Logo prompt: "Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished, distinctive professional logo symbol for Prism, a premium native macOS screen recording and video editing application. Deliver a single centered logo mark on a genuinely transparent background, generous clear space, no wordmark, no letters written as text, no mockup, no logo sheet. Concept: a geometrically precise folded optical prism that subtly forms a capital P and suggests a captured screen/aperture. A broad solid vertical stem and a faceted rounded rectangular upper aperture, with one clean angular refraction cut and a generous negative-space opening. It must be recognizable as one coherent proprietary symbol, not a generic record-circle, camera, play triangle, sparkle, or stacked hexagons. Two main colors: rich professional cobalt blue #346BEB and sea green #35BCA5, with a darker sapphire facet #2350A5. Favor sophisticated mostly flat solid geometry with only very subtle tonal facet separation; no rainbow, neon glow, chrome, glass blur, grain, heavy shadows, hairline details, or decorative dots. Exact clean edges, intentional proportions, optically balanced, premium software brand aesthetic. The silhouette must remain strong at 24 pixels. Large centered mark occupies about 72 percent of a square canvas. Transparent alpha outside the logo."

App icon prompt, using the logo as the reference: "Use case: logo-brand. Convert the provided Prism logo symbol into a finished premium macOS application icon. Preserve the exact folded P silhouette, generous negative space, and cobalt/sea-green facet arrangement of the reference; clean any stray alpha specks. Center that mark on a deep ink/navy #101D32 rounded-square macOS tile. The tile occupies 88 percent of a square canvas with a consistent transparent margin; its corner radius is about 22 percent of tile width. The P logo has height about 62 percent of the overall canvas, optically centered slightly above center, with comfortable clear space on every side. Rich cobalt #346BEB, restrained teal #35BCA5, darker sapphire fold. Only a very subtle top-left edge highlight and soft grounded tile shadow, premium precise finish. Flat graphic brand mark, no glass blur, no neon bloom, no metallic extrusions, no rainbow, no additional symbols, no text, no badges. One single front-facing square app icon on genuinely transparent background outside the rounded tile. Render clean enough for a 1024-pixel macOS icon master and recognizable at 32 pixels."
